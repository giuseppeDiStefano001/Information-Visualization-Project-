FIDE Chess Demographics analysis

Question: How has the demographic composition of the global ranking changed in recent years?

Year slider:
The user drags the slider to advance the timeline from one year to the other. The bars will smoothly extend or retract their length to reflect the changing percentage of players per gender over the years.

Age range slider:
A two-sided slider which the user can use to show only the interval of ages wanted (e.g., 30-50). There will be a zoom-in or zoom-out on the selected age interval of the chess players.

Age range click:
If one of the age ranges on the Y-axis is selected, there will be a zoom-in on that range. The Y-axis will show the range selected by the previous click, revealing the granular distribution for individual years within that range.
